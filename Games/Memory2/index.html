How To Play

When you click the start button all the small tiles will be revealed. You will have 5 seconds to memorize the images. When time is up all small tiles will flip back over and the large tile will be revealed. Take turns flipping a single small tile. Tap a tile to flip them over. If it matches the big tile give a drink and go again, if they differ take a drink and next players turn. The big tile will then change to a new image. (sometimes there are more than 1 matching set)

Time, gameboard size, and drinking rules can be adjusted for prefered experience.
